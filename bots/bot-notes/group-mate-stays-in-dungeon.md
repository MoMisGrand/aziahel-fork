Fixed: A bot following its group walked back out of the dungeon over a side quest
When a bot joined its group mate inside a dungeon and both had a quest whose next step was outside, the bot walked straight back out through the exit, then teleported back in, over and over. Now a shared quest step that leaves the area is left to the group mate to lead, and the bot stays with them.
Where: Any dungeon while bots are grouped, first seen with Idunn and Cyndr in the Tomb of Storms.
Test: Group two bots that share a side quest, have one go into a dungeon, and check the other follows and stays inside with them.
