Fixed: A bot stuck where another bot already got free now tries that bot's fix first
Each bot kept its own book of stuck spots and what freed it there, and other bots never read it. Mythis got out of a dead end in The Tower Archives by taking another teleporter. Kitharas Shade later got stuck at the same spot and found nothing written down. It pressed the Commons button and walked straight back into the dead end, over and over for more than two hours on "The Towering Crystal". Now, when a bot has nothing that worked at a spot, it first tries what freed another bot there: Ultimus, the TCO7 team or the Kitharas Family, not the scouts.
Where: every stuck spot for Ultimus, the TCO7 team and the Kitharas Family; each bot's spots are in abnormal-fixes.json in its log folder.
Test: watch Kitharas Shade on "The Towering Crystal" in The Tower Archives. When it stops near (6846, -1015), it should take another way (the teleporter Mythis used) instead of pressing the Commons button, then reach the Yellow Crystal.
