Fixed: Ultimus was sent back to the start while resting on purpose
When Ultimus sets a step aside and has nothing else to do, he rests for a few minutes before trying it again. His "no progress" timer counted that rest as being stuck, pressed the Commons button on him, and the walk back put him at the start of the Tower Archives, far from where he had got to. A planned rest now keeps that timer quiet; the rest itself ends after 5 minutes.
Where: Dragonspyre, The Tower Archives ("Portal Combat"), and anywhere a bot rests from a step.
