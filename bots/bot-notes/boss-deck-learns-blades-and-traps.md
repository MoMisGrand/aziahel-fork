Changed: Ultimus and the teams build a separate deck mix for solo bosses
Against a boss met alone, a bot's damage comes from blades and traps stacked under one big hit. Before, every fight fed one shared mix, and crowd side fights pushed it to three quarters hits. So Ultimus's deck against Vladimir Darkflame had only two Bladestorm and one Hex, and he could stack just one blade under each Judgement. Now boss fights have their own share of hits, starting at the normal 60%. When a boss outlasts the bot, the share moves down and more blades and traps go in, while crowd fights still learn as before. A rival boss that outlasts the bot now also gets blades and traps instead of more plain hits.
Where: deck building for Ultimus, the TCO7 team and the Kitharas Family before any boss met alone; the numbers are in deck-mix.txt in each bot's log folder.
