Fixed: Bots sharing a dungeon no longer undo each other on any puzzle
The plaque fix for Katzenstein's Lab now covers every dungeon puzzle made of plates, plaques, obelisks or braziers, not only the ones done in order. Each piece turns every time a wizard steps on or pulls it, so two bots on the same puzzle turned pieces back, as with the moons in the Temple of Storms. Now one bot sets the puzzle and the others stand clear until it is done.
Where: Every dungeon puzzle (Temple of Storms moons, Vault of Ice obelisks and braziers, and others), no setting
Test: Send two bots into the same Temple of Storms copy and check that one sets the moons while the other waits.
