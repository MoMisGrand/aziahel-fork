Fixed: Bots walked through the same wrong door again and again

When a bot's walk took it through a door that goes somewhere else, it backed out and then set off through the same door again. Kitharas Hydra went in and out of Merle Ambrose's Tower nine times in three minutes on the way to Nightside. The spot by a door that went elsewhere is now marked, so every bot's walk bends round it from then on.

Where: Ultimus and every bot built on him, in any area with doors close to their path (The Commons most of all)
Test: Watch a bot walk from The Commons to Nightside or the Shopping District: it never ends up in Merle Ambrose's Tower on the way.
