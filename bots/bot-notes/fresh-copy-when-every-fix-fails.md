Fixed: Bots stuck behind a shut dungeon door start the dungeon over
When a bot has tried every fix twice over inside a dungeon and made no real progress for 20 minutes, it now walks out and back in for a fresh copy, as a player would. Garm and Ra stood behind a shut door in Big Ben for over an hour, because the creature that opens it never came out in their copy. A fresh copy brings the doors and creatures back.
Where: any dungeon, first seen behind the fourth room's door in Big Ben.
Test: a bot stuck behind a shut door in a dungeon walks out and back in after about 20 minutes.
