Fixed: Running out of cards in a long boss fight
In a tough fight with a full hand, a bot threw a card away every round to make room for a treasure card. In an 18-minute fight with Vladimir Darkflame Ultimus threw away a card every round, his deck ran dry by round 31, and he ended on tiny hits while the boss healed back up. Now a bot only throws a card away for a treasure card while its deck still has at least 10 cards, or when its hand has no hit left.
Where: Any long fight, most of all bosses that heal, like Vladimir Darkflame in The Labyrinth.
Test: Watch a long boss fight: once the deck is under 10 cards the bot stops throwing cards away for treasure cards.
