Fixed: Bots cast damage globals for their big hit, and count the global already up when they work out a hit.
A global (Darkwind, Supercharge and the like) boosts a school's hits for the whole fight, for the bot and its team. The bots had no idea what one was. Now a global for the big hit's school is part of the stack-up plan. It is played only when it beats the global already up and the enemies don't hit with that school, since it boosts them too. The treasure card plan buys one or two that fit the bot's school, and every damage estimate (including the "Judgement kills at N pips" sum) counts the global on the battlefield. An X-pip setup card such as Fire's Backdraft takes every pip, so it is only played while the bot has 2 pips or fewer, leaving the finisher's pips alone.
