Fixed: A bot that picks a teleporter to get unstuck now gets to use it
When a bot makes no progress for a few minutes it tries a teleporter that lands nearer its goal. At the same moment another check could decide the bot was going round in circles and send it off to rest, so the teleporter was never reached. Now that check waits three minutes while a teleporter fix is under way. Ultimus lost his way out of the north end of the Tower Archives to this at 2:28 AM on 21 November, although the same teleporter had taken him close to his goal at 12:45 AM.
Where: Every area, seen in Dragonspyre's Tower Archives on Portal Combat.
Test: Watch Ultimus's thoughts in the Tower Archives: after "tries another way, the teleporter at", he should press it instead of resting.
