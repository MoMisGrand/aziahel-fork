Changed: Bots save their pips for a big hit instead of tapping
In fights against tough creatures, a bot playing "hit at once" spent its pips round after round on small hits that barely dented the enemy, while a hit two or three times as big sat in its hand waiting for pips. Ultimus took 13 rounds, about 7 minutes, over one side fight in the Tower Archives this way. Now, when its only castable hit takes less than a quarter of the enemy's health and a much bigger hit is waiting, the bot builds pips or blades first, then casts the big one.
Where: every fight for Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Kitharas Family, most of all in Dragonspyre and later worlds
Test: watch a bot fight two Dragonspyre creatures and see it pass or blade, then cast its biggest hit, instead of casting 1-pip hits every round
