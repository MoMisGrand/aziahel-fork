Fixed: Every bot now knows how school pips work and turns its Power Orb in battle to the school its cards need. Some cards cost a school pip, like Celestial Intervention's treasure card, which needs an Ice pip. School pips come only from the Power Orb (Archmastery), which a wizard gets at level 50, or earlier from Archmastery on gear, jewels or pets. Each round the orb turns power pips into pips of the school it is set to, and a click on the orb changes that school. Now, when a card in hand needs another school's pips, the bot turns its orb to that school, then turns it back to its own school once nothing needs it. It keeps its own school otherwise, because its own school's pips count as power pips for its own spells. Below level 50 with no Archmastery, it no longer plans or buys cards that cost school pips, since it can never cast them.

Where: Ultimus, the Calling of 7, the Kitharas Family, the Quest Team and the Bot Players, in every battle (True Form and Zoom keep their own setup).
