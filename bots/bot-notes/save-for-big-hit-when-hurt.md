Fixed: Bots spent their pips on tiny Judgements while a little hurt
When a bot was a bit hurt in a long boss fight and had no heal or shield to play, it cast its pip-hungry hit at two or three pips for very little damage, round after round, instead of building up for the next big hit. Now it keeps saving for the full-pip hit unless its health is really low. Ultimus did this against Vladimir Darkflame, casting Judgement for about 288 and 144 into a boss with more than 3,600 health left.
Where: any boss fight for Ultimus, the TCO7 team and the Kitharas Family.
Test: watch Ultimus fight Vladimir Darkflame in The Labyrinth; after his first big Judgement he should save pips again instead of casting small Judgements while above about a third of his health.
