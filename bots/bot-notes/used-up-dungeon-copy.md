Fixed: Bots stood for hours by things already used up in a dungeon
When a dungeon's things to use for a quest step were already used in that copy, for example after a restart started the dungeon's quest over, the bots kept walking between the empty spots. Now, once nothing is left to use for 20 minutes with no story progress, the bot walks out and back in for a fresh copy, as a player does.
Where: any dungeon with things to collect or use, first seen with Collect Crates in Katzenstein's Lab on If You Build It.
Test: in Katzenstein's Lab with Collect Crates open and the crates gone, the bot leaves and comes back in to a fresh copy within about 20 minutes and collects the crates.
