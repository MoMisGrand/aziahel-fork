Fixed: Bots bring a bigger deck to a boss that keeps outlasting them
Against a boss whose fights run very long, a bot's small deck ran dry before the boss fell, so it stood passing turns while the boss healed back up. Ultimus lost to the Jade Oni twice this way, with the Oni down to about a third of its health both times. Now, against a boss that has beaten a bot and whose fights run long, the bot packs about eight more cards, so it still has hits to play late in the fight.
Where: Any long boss fight a bot has lost before, first seen in MooShu's Jade Palace (quest An Imperial Cure, Defeat Jade Oni).
Test: Watch Ultimus go back to the Jade Oni and check that his deck note lists more cards than before and that he keeps casting to the end of the fight.
