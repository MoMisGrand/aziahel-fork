Changed: Bots drink a potion before a dungeon fight when under a quarter of their health
In a dungeon, a bot that is under a quarter of its health with a fight next now drinks a potion first, even the last one it was saving for the boss. Going into a dungeon fight that low almost always loses it, and a loss there sends the bot back by Dungeon Recall or into a new copy, which costs far more than a potion. Above a quarter of their health, bots still keep their potions for right before the boss.
Where: Every dungeon, for Ultimus, TCO7 and the Kitharas Family.
Test: Watch a bot in a dungeon finish a fight with under a quarter of its health and a potion left: before the next fight it should drink one and say so.
