Fixed: Bots no longer give up a room's battle circle the moment they walk into the next room
In a dungeon whose rooms each have a battle circle with the same name, like Lord Obsidian's Tomb, a bot kept timing its walk to "the circle" from the first room onward. Five minutes after the first room's fight, it walked into the fourth room and gave up that room's circle at once ("no way there in 5 minutes"). That circle brings out the Banshee whose defeat opens the gauntlet door, so the door stayed shut and the bot stood at it, then pressed the Commons button and started the dungeon over, only to get stuck at the same door again. Now the five minutes start again in each new room, and again after a break of two minutes or more in the walk.
Where: every dungeon with a battle circle per room (Lord Obsidian's Tomb, "Obsidian in Crystal"), for Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Kitharas Family.
Test: Watch Ultimus in Lord Obsidian's Tomb: in each room he steps on the battle circle and fights, and after the Banshee fight the gauntlet door opens. The log should not show "gives up on the room's battle circle" right after "is in Lord Obsidian's Tomb".
