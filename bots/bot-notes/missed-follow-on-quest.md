Fixed: Bots that miss the next quest's offer go back and get it
In Marleybone the story goes on with "Fetch Bones!", but the game only offers it right when "Weird Science" is handed in. A bot that missed that offer thought no story quest was left and wandered off to Wizard City. Now, when no story quest is offered, the bot looks for a main quest in the furthest world it has reached and goes to its giver, so it gets Fetch Bones! from Watson.
Where: Marleybone after Weird Science, and any world whose story goes on with quests the game does not mark as story.
Test: a bot that finished Weird Science without taking Fetch Bones! walks back to Watson in Scotland Yard and takes it.
