Fixed: Bots threw away their heal cards to make room for a treasure card in hard fights.

To draw a treasure card with a full hand, a bot throws one card away first. It sometimes picked a spare heal: Ultimus threw away Satyr against the Sea Lord, and another bot threw away Fairy at under half health, then had nothing to heal with. Now a heal is never the card thrown away for a treasure card. A charm, ward or extra hit goes instead.

Where: Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Kitharas in hard fights.
Test: Watch Ultimus fight the Sea Lord. His notes "discards ... to make room for a treasure card" should never name Satyr or another heal.
