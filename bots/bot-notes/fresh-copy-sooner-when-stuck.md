Changed: A bot stuck in a dungeon starts it over in minutes, not twenty
When a bot has tried every fix twice over inside a dungeon, it now walks out and back in about 5 minutes later. It used to also wait 20 minutes with no progress, and each bots update started those 20 minutes over, so it never happened. Trying every fix twice already takes a good while, so the extra wait is short.
Where: any dungeon, first seen behind the fourth room's door in Big Ben.
Test: a bot stuck behind a shut door in Big Ben walks out and back in soon after it says it needs help.
