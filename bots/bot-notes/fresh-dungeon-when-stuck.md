New: Bots start a dungeon over when the creature they need never shows up to fight
If the creature a bot's quest needs inside a dungeon has not been there to fight for 20 minutes, and the bot has made no story progress in that time, it now walks out the way it came in and comes back, so the dungeon starts fresh, as a player would. It never does this in the middle of a fight, or while it is grouped with a player.
Where: Every dungeon, first needed for Pops O'Leary in The Ironworks in Marleybone.
Test: Watch a bot that has stood in a dungeon for 20 minutes with nothing to fight walk out, come back in, and find the creature it needs.
