Fixed: Going back to a boss that beat him at too little health
After losing in a dungeon, a bot went back in by Dungeon Recall once it had 60% of its health, even when the boss there had beaten it before. Ultimus met Vladimir Darkflame at 61% this way and lost again. Now, when the fight it lost was against something that has beaten it before, it heals up to its tough-fight health first, and only goes back in sooner when the Dungeon Recall is about to run out.
Where: Dragonspyre, The Labyrinth (Stay for Detention), and any dungeon boss a bot keeps losing to.
Test: Let a bot lose to a dungeon boss it has lost to before, then watch it heal to nearly full before it uses Dungeon Recall.
