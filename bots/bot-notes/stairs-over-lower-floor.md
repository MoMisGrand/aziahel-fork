Fixed: Bots walk up long stairs instead of staying on the floor underneath
Where the game's map has a hidden floor under a staircase or ramp, the bots kept their feet on that hidden floor and never climbed. In the Jade Palace this left Ultimus far below the throne room, so the Jade Oni never started a fight with him and he kept giving up on An Imperial Cure. Bots now take any step up they could walk, so they climb stairs and ramps the way a player does, while a balcony high above a hall is still left alone.
Where: Every area with stairs or a ramp over a lower floor, first seen in MooShu's Jade Palace (quest An Imperial Cure, Defeat Jade Oni).
Test: Send a bot into the Jade Palace on An Imperial Cure and watch it walk up the long stairs and start the Jade Oni fight at the throne.
