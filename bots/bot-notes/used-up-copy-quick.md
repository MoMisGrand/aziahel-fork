Changed: A used-up dungeon copy is started over in minutes
When everything a step needs at its spot in a dungeon is already used, the bot now walks out and back in after about 3 minutes instead of 20. The 20 minutes started over at every bots update, so a bot could stand by used crates for hours. A dungeon still gets at most two quick fresh copies an hour.
Where: any dungeon with things to collect or use, first seen with Collect Crates in Katzenstein's Lab.
Test: in Katzenstein's Lab with the crates used up, the bot walks out and back in within about 3 minutes.
