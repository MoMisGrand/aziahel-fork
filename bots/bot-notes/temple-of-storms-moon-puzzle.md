Fixed: The bots could not finish the moon and sun puzzle in the Temple of Storms
In this puzzle, lighting some moons puts other moons out again. The bots lit them in an order where a later moon kept putting an earlier one out, so the door to the Son of Storms never opened and four of them tried for over an hour. Now the bots light first the pieces that put others out, then the rest, so every moon stays lit and the suns go dark.
Where: The Temple of Storms in Krokotopia, on the quest "Test of Strength", and any puzzle where one piece undoes another.
