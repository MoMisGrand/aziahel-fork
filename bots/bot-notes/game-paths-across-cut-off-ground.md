Fixed: Ultimus wandered off the wrong way when his map showed his goal cut off
In the Tower Archives in Dragonspyre, Ultimus kept walking far to the north on his way to the teleporter up the tower and never got there. His map of the ground shows that teleporter's floor apart from the entrance, so he guessed a way that went nowhere. Now, when his map shows the spot he is walking to cut off from where he stands, he follows the game's own walking paths instead, which go down and back up to it. The bots built on him get the same help.
Where: Dragonspyre, Tower Archives, and any area with floors the map shows apart.
Test: Watch Ultimus on Portal Combat in the Tower Archives and see him walk straight to the teleporter up the tower instead of heading north.
