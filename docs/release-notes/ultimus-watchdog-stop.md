New: Ultimus stops himself after two hours with nothing done

If Ultimus goes two hours without finishing a step or gaining any XP, he now pauses himself instead of going round the same spot all night. A red note shows at the top of the Ultimus page in Ahziael saying where he stopped and what he was doing. Press Copy what he was doing and paste it in the chat so the spot can be fixed, then press Resume him to let him go on.

Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page.
Test: Nothing to set up. If Ultimus ever gets stuck for two hours, open the Ultimus page and look for the red note with its two buttons.
