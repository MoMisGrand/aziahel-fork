New: The Quest Team changes its approach after ten alarms on one step
Like Ultimus, when the team gets stuck ten times at one spot on the same step, it stops repeating the same moves. If there are creatures around, it fights them first and then tries the step again. If there are none, it takes a two-minute break and finds its way again fresh.
Where: The Quest Team, no setting.
Test: Let the team play a long while. In its log you see "NEW APPROACH" if this happens, followed by a fight or a short break.
