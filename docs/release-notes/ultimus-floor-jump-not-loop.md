Fixed: Ultimus sitting down for 5 minutes right after going up a floor in Big Ben
After Dungeon Recall put Ultimus back into a Big Ben copy he had climbed before, he went straight up the floors he had already beaten. On floors 5, 8 and 11, his loop guard counted that floor's earlier tries, decided he was going in circles, and sat him down for 5 minutes, even though the teleporter had just taken him up. Now going up a floor by its teleporter counts as progress, so the guard only steps in when he really is stuck on a floor.
Where: Big Ben and every dungeon with floors joined by teleporters
Test: After Ultimus falls in Big Ben and comes back by Dungeon Recall, check that he goes up the floors he already beat without sitting down to rest between them.
