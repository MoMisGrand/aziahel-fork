Fixed: A boss you summoned comes back after a server restart
In Dragonspyre's Plaza of Conquests, the Summoning Crystal brings out the Earth Elemental or the Sea Lord. A server restart sent the boss away, but your quest still counted the crystal as used, so the boss never came back and the "Defeat" step could not be done. Now, when your step is to defeat one such boss and it is missing, it comes back when you enter the area, as if the crystal had been used again.
Where: Dragonspyre, Plaza of Conquests ("No Weaklings Allowed", "Foe of Foes" and the other crystal fights), and any area where an earlier step summons a single boss.
Test: Use the crystal for "Foe of Foes", let the server restart before the fight, then come back to the Plaza: the Sea Lord should be standing at its spot again.
