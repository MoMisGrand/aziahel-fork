Fixed: A wizard stuck in a closed shop or dungeon room after walking straight back in

When someone left a room such as a shop or a dungeon room and walked right back in, the room could close in the moment their game was loading it. They then stood in a room that no longer worked: no exit took them out, and friends saw them as offline. This happened to Idunn in the Wizard City Robe Shop for 18 minutes. Now a room waits for anyone on their way in before it closes.

Where: Any shop, house interior or dungeon room, for players and bots.
Test: Walk out of the Wizard City Robe Shop and straight back in a few times, then check that the exit still works and friends still see you online.
