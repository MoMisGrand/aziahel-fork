Fixed: Ultimus saves his potion for the quest's own boss
Ultimus drank his saved potion before a room fight that the game marks as a boss, so it was gone when he reached the real boss. Now he only drinks it right before the boss his quest step names, not before room fights, fights while a step waits, or a group mate's fight.
Where: Dungeons with boss-marked room creatures, such as Gearhead in Big Ben.
