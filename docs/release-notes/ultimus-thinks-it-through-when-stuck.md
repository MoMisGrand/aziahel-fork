New: Ultimus thinks it through when he is stuck
When Ultimus gets stuck on the same step again, he first does what got him going there before. If nothing has worked there yet, the local AI on your PC reads the step, what the game told him, what is around him and what he already tried, guesses why he is stuck and picks one of his normal moves: step aside, walk away and back, another route, the Commons button, fight what is around, click what is offered, or set the step aside for a while. He checks whether it worked and remembers it, and the Quest Team reads what he learned. He never skips or finishes a step this way, and without the AI app running he still uses what worked before.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus, Ultimus AI Chat page, the "Think it through when stuck" switch (on by default, changes count right away). In Imlight.ini: [Ultimus] StuckThinking. His reasoning shows in Ultimus Thoughts and his log.
Test: With Ollama running and the address and model set on that page, let Ultimus quest until he gets stuck twice in one spot. His thoughts should say "I thought it through", give a guess at the cause and the move, and later say whether it got him going.
