New: Read-ahead lists puzzle steps
When Ultimus or the Quest Team read ahead before a new world, they now also list the story quests there that have a puzzle, a riddle or things to use in order, and whether it was solved before. The list is written to puzzles-ahead.txt in their folder. Mini-games are flagged too, though no story quest in the game files needs one.
Where: Ultimus and Quest Team read-ahead, no setting.
Test: Start a read-ahead for Marleybone. The log and puzzles-ahead.txt name Cavern of the Wurm as a puzzle step.
