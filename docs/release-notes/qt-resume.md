New: The Quest Team picks up where it left off
After a server restart, or turning the team off and on, the team carries on from where it was. It keeps how long each story quest has taken, its alarms and lost battles on each step, any short break, and the collect spots each wizard already did. Restart in Ahziael still starts four new wizards fresh.
Where: The Quest Team, no setting.
Test: Let the team play a few minutes, then turn it off and on in Ahziael. Its log says it picks up where it left off, naming the quest it was on.
