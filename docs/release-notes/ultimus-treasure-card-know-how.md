Fixed: Ultimus knows which treasure cards to use, when, and where to buy them

Ultimus now judges each treasure card he owns by the fights ahead: big hits the coming creatures are weak to, heals, shields against the schools they hit with, blades for his own school, and stuns or weakness for bosses. The best ones go into his deck first, and cards that can't help him stay in his book. When he is down to a few cards with a hard fight ahead, like a dungeon or a boss that beat him, he goes to the nearest game vendor in his world that sells cards he can use, stocks up and Recalls back. He never uses the Bazaar for this. In battle he keeps his treasure cards when the fight is already nearly won. He writes all of this down in treasure-cards.txt: the cards he holds, when he plays each one, and which vendors in his world sell what, with prices.
Where: Ultimus and the TCO7, in any world; the notes are in the Ultimus logs folder (each TCO7 member in their own folder).
Test: Let Ultimus run low on treasure cards before Big Ben; he should travel to a Marleybone treasure card vendor, buy cards that fit the fights ahead, and Recall back. Open treasure-cards.txt to see his notes.
