Fixed: Ultimus skipping Big Ben's fights and walking through shut doors
In Big Ben, after Ultimus talked to Travis Pawman in the first room, he thought the second Travis Pawman on floor 5 was on a floor behind him. That turned off his floor fights, which are only for the way up. Our map of Big Ben has no doors in it, so he then walked straight through the shut doors of rooms 1 to 3 and got stuck on room 3's elevator, which won't work until the floors are beaten. Now he only counts a step as behind him when the way there is one of the area's own teleporters back, so he plays each floor as a player would. And no bot walk goes through a door that is still shut in its copy of the area: it stops at the door, as a player's game would stop them.
Where: Big Ben and every dungeon with doors that open later, for Ultimus, the TCO7 bots and the Quest Team
Test: Send Ultimus into Big Ben and check that he fights room 1 before going through its door, and that he never shows up past a door that is still shut.
