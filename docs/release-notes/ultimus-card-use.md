Fixed: Ultimus learns which cards he really plays

Ultimus and the TCO7 now count, for every card, how many fights it sat in their hand and how many times they cast it. A card that was in their hand in 5 or more fights and never cast is set aside as rarely played: an item card is turned off, a treasure card stays in the book, and a spell of the main deck makes way for another. Heals are always kept, and a set aside card gets a new try every 5 levels. X pip spells are now judged by the pips they really have that round instead of their biggest tier, and damage per pip spells by the enemy's pips.

Where: card-use.txt in Ultimus's notes folder (and each TCO7 member's own folder), which also lists his deck with why each spell is in it.
Test: let Ultimus fight a few battles, then open card-use.txt and check that each card shows how often it was held and cast, and that a card he never plays is marked rarely played.
