New: Bots remember which quest items need a fight first

When Ultimus or the Quest Team had to beat the creatures by a quest item before it worked, that is now remembered. The next time any of them reaches that item with creatures by it, they fight first instead of trying the item and failing. This is kept through restarts and updates.

Where: Ultimus and the Quest Team, any "use" quest step.
Test: After Ultimus has done the Frozen Forge once, let another bot reach it. Its log should say it remembers the forge needed the creatures beaten first.
