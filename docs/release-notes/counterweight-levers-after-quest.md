Fixed: Counterweight East levers after Metal Madness
The levers in Counterweight East could only be pulled while Metal Madness asked for them. After that quest was done, a fresh copy of the dungeon kept its first gate shut and the lever offered nothing, so Stolen Away could never reach Sprockets. Now a lever that opens a dungeon's gates can be pulled by anyone, quest or not, the way it works in the game.
Where: Marleybone, Big Ben, Counterweight East (Stolen Away).
Test: With Metal Madness already done, go into Counterweight East on Stolen Away and pull the first lever; the gate should open and the way up to Sprockets should work.
