Fixed: Ultimus not following you into Meowiarty's room

When you led Ultimus up Big Ben, he followed you to floor 20 but stopped at the open door, just short of the teleporter to Meowiarty's room. Our map of Big Ben is drawn with every door shut, so his walk to the teleporter ended at the door even though it was open. Now, when a teleporter he heads for sits just past a door that is open, he walks straight through the door onto it. This works whether he follows you or goes on his own.

Where: Big Ben floor 20 (Door21 and the teleporter to Meowiarty), and any dungeon door with a teleporter just past it.
Test: Lead Ultimus through Big Ben to floor 20, beat the room, walk onto the boss teleporter, and check that he comes into Meowiarty's room after you.
