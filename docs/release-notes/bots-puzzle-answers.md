New: Ultimus and the Quest Team pick puzzle answers from the quest's words
When an NPC in a puzzle quest offers more than one answer, Ultimus and the Quest Team first pick the answer that best matches what the quest's people said. If it is wrong, they try the next one. The answer that worked is written down for next time.
Where: Ultimus and Quest Team, no setting.
Test: Watch a riddle quest such as The Auroracle's Riddle. If an NPC offers several answers, the log shows which one was picked first.
