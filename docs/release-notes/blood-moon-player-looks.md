New: See every wizard in a zone in Blood Moon
Your server now notes how each wizard looks and what gear they wear, so Blood Moon can show every wizard standing in a zone, where they are and which way they face, with their outfit listed piece by piece. The server only writes what it already knows; nothing changes in the game.
Where: Blood Moon, the Players layer, on your own server.
Test: Log in with a wizard, open the same zone in Blood Moon and check your wizard shows up where you stand with the gear you wear.
