New: Move the game's own NPCs live from Blood Moon
Blood Moon, the 3D zone viewer, can now move, turn and resize the game's own NPCs and objects on your server while it runs. Drag an NPC to a new spot in Blood Moon with "Live to server" on, and it moves in the game straight away for everyone in that zone; "Put back where the game has it" sends it home again. The moves are saved on your server, so they stay after a restart, and the game's files are never changed. Battle circles and dungeon sigil pads stay where they are for now.
Where: Blood Moon, Move and Live to server, on your own server.
Test: Open the Commons in Blood Moon, turn on Live to server, drag the potion shopkeeper a few steps and check that she moves in the game without a restart.
