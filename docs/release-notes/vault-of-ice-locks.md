Fixed: Bots open the three locks in Krokotopia's Vault of Ice

In the Vault of Ice, the Frost door to the Keeper of the Fang has three locks, and each one opens from a puzzle in its own wing. Bots never knew this, so Tulkar, Ra, Kurrent and Mythis stood at the shut Bird lock for hours. Now a bot that finds a lock shut goes to that wing's battle circle and beats the guardian. Then it clicks the pieces the way a player does: the four obelisks in order (Snake, Moon, Bird, Sun), the two obelisks in the lever room, and the three braziers set to Fire, Ice and Storm.
Where: Krokotopia, Krokosphinx, The Vault of Ice, for Ultimus, the Quest Team and the Calling of 7.
