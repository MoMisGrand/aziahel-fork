Fixed: Bots no longer get pulled into the trainer hall
The trainer hall door in the Commons sits on the path to the Shopping District. Every time a bot walked over it, the server tried to send the bot into the trainer hall, which a bot cannot load, so the bot stood still for a minute and a half until "The house could not be prepared" came up. This happened 43 times on the night of 21 November. The door now lets bots walk past, and it still works for people exactly as before.
Where: The Commons, the trainer hall door spot on the way to the Shopping District.
Test: Walk onto the trainer hall door yourself and you still go to the hall; watch a bot walk to the Shopping District and it goes straight through.
