New: The Quest Team writes down what gets it unstuck
When the Quest Team gets stuck, it now writes down the move it made (stepping aside or walking well away) and whether that got it going again. Ultimus and the team share this memory, so whichever one gets stuck there next tries what worked first.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, Stuck solutions (lines marked "By Quest Team").
Test: Turn the Quest Team on and let it play. After it gets stuck and moves on, Stuck solutions shows a line by Quest Team.
