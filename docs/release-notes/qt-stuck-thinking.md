New: The Quest Team thinks a stuck spot through with the local AI
When the team is stuck on the same step again, it now asks the same local AI Ultimus uses, on your PC. The AI guesses the cause and picks one move a player would make, such as stepping aside, taking another way, fighting nearby creatures or clicking what is offered. It never skips or finishes a step, and it uses the same Stuck Thinking switch as Ultimus.
Where: The Quest Team, using the Stuck Thinking switch on Ahziael's Ultimus AI Chat page.
Test: With the local AI running, watch the team's log when it gets stuck twice in one spot. You should see "THOUGHT IT THROUGH" with a cause and the move it makes.
