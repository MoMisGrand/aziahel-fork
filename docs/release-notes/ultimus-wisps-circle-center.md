Fixed: Ultimus picks up the wisps a battle leaves before his next dungeon fight
After a battle, Ultimus used to head straight for the next floor duty, so the wisps that fall in the middle of the battle circle disappeared before he got to them. Now, if he is not at full health or mana after a win, he goes to the middle of the circle first, halfway between his spot and the first enemy's spot, and walks through the wisps as they fall. Then he goes on to the next fight.
Where: Ultimus after every battle he wins, including dungeon floors such as Big Ben's.
Test: Watch Ultimus win a floor fight in Big Ben while hurt, and check he walks to the middle of the circle and picks up its wisps before the next fight.
