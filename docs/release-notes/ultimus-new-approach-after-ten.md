New: Ultimus changes his approach after getting stuck 10 times in one place

When Ultimus got stuck at the same spot on the same step, he used to repeat the same few moves, 75 times at the Frozen Forge. Now, on the 10th time, he does something different. If there are creatures around him, he fights them first and then tries his step again. If there are none, he sets the step aside for a while and does other story work first.
