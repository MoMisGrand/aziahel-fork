Fixed: A street boss could stop coming back
On a shared street a creature could be gone without the area noticing, and then its spot never brought a new one. King Uro Ahnic in the Ahnic Family Tomb did this on 21 November: he was beaten at 2:48 AM and never came back, while four players waited for him on "De-Cipher the Ahnics". Now a spot whose creature is gone fills again on its normal timer, and the server log says when it had to do this.
Where: Krokotopia, Ahnic Family Tomb (and every shared street area).
Test: Beat King Uro Ahnic in the Ahnic Family Tomb, wait a few minutes, and check that he is back.
