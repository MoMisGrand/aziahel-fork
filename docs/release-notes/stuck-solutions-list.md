New: See and clear what Ultimus and the Quest Team learned when stuck
The Ultimus page has a new Stuck solutions list. Each line shows the quest step, the area, Ultimus's guess at why he was stuck, the move that was tried, how often it worked or failed, and who tried it. Click Forget on a wrong line, for example after a quest was fixed, or Forget everything to start fresh. It works with the server on or off.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, Stuck solutions.
Test: Open the Ultimus page and find Stuck solutions. Click Forget on a line and it is gone within a few seconds.
