Fixed: The arches in the Dragonspyre Basilica stopped taking you anywhere
After the last update, pressing X at the arches in the Basilica, like the one to The Atheneum, did nothing. The fix for the arch menu kept the arch's light-up action, which has no destination, and dropped the part that actually takes you through. Now an arch keeps the light-up action only when it knows where to send you, and otherwise it keeps the working door, still with no menu.
Where: Dragonspyre, The Basilica arches to The Atheneum, the Academy and the other wings, and any arch like them.
Test: In The Basilica, walk up to the arch to The Atheneum and press X. You go straight through with no menu.
