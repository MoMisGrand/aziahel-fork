New: Healing and mana radiance cantrips work
The Healing, Mana and Healing and Mana cantrips on the Cantrips tab now heal you over time outside battle, for their usual energy. Level I gives back 10% over about a minute, II 20%, III 30% and IV 40%, and battle pauses it. Fishing luck radiances still do nothing yet.
Where: Any wizard, Cantrips tab. Setting: Cantrips RadianceShare in Imlight.ini (level I's share, default 0.10).
Test: Get hurt in a battle, then cast Healing IV from the Cantrips tab. Your health goes up a little every few seconds and your energy goes down.
