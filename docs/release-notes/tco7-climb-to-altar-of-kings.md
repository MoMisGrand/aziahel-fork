Fixed: Bots standing under a raised step and never climbing to it
The TCO7 stood for hours at the bottom of the Oasis, right under Professor Winthrop by the door to the Altar of Kings, which is on a raised floor above them. Because they were standing almost exactly under him, the way finder thought they had already arrived and never looked for the stairs. Now, when a bot is right under a step on a higher or lower floor, it uses the game's own walk map to take the stairs up or down to it.
Where: Krokotopia, the Oasis, and any area where a quest step is on a floor right above or below a bot
Test: Send a TCO7 bot to Professor Winthrop at the Oasis from the floor below, and check that it walks round by the stairs and reaches him.
