Fixed: Bots stood on the Keymaster's door pad in Emperor's Retreat

In Krokotopia's Emperor's Retreat, the pad by the Keymaster's door only opens it after the Colossus fight in the fourth tower is done, but the TCO7 kept stepping on it for an hour and tried to reach the obelisks behind the shut door. Bots now read when a door waits on a dungeon quest, leave its pad and the puzzles behind it alone until that quest is done, and follow their quest instead.

Where: Emperor's Retreat in Krokotopia, and any dungeon door that opens only after one of the dungeon's own quests.
Test: have a TCO7 member reach the Keymaster's door before the Colossus and check that he goes on with his quest instead of standing on the pad.
