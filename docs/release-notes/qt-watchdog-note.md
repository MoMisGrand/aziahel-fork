New: A red note on the Quest Team page when the team stopped itself
When the Quest Team pauses itself after two hours with nothing done, the Quest Team page in Ahziael shows a red note saying so. It has two buttons. "Copy what they were doing" copies the details for you to paste in the chat. "Resume them" starts the team again.
Where: Ahziael, Quest Team page.
