Fixed: Ultimus no longer sits down at the boss teleporter after coming back into a dungeon

After losing to a dungeon boss and coming back by Dungeon Recall, Ultimus climbed back up to the boss teleporter and then rested for five minutes, because the loop guard was still counting his tries from before the fight. Coming back into a dungeon now starts its floors fresh, and stepping on and off a teleporter that has not fired yet counts as one try instead of one try every second.
Where: Ultimus in any dungeon with floors, such as Big Ben in Marleybone.
Test: Let Ultimus lose to Meowiarty in Big Ben and come back by Dungeon Recall, then watch that he goes straight onto the boss teleporter without a "LOOP" line in his log.
