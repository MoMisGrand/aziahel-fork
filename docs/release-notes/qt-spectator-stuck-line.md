New: The Quest Team Spectator shows a "Team stuck" line
When the team is stuck, the Quest Team Spectator now shows a "Team stuck" line. It says where the team is, its height, what it was doing and what it is trying next. The line goes away as soon as the team makes progress again.
Where: The Quest Team Spectator add-on.
Test: Watch the team in the Quest Team Spectator. If it gets stuck, a "Team stuck" line shows under its quest.
