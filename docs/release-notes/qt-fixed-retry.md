New: Fixed it and Retry now buttons on Quest Team issues
Each quest issue on Team Issues in Ahziael now has two buttons, like Ultimus's. "Fixed it" tells the team to forget what went wrong with that quest and try it again from the start. "Retry now" makes the team stop its break or wait and try its step again at once.
Where: Ahziael, Quest Team page, Team Issues.
Test: Open Team Issues and press Retry now on any quest issue. The team's log says "RETRY NOW" within a few seconds.
