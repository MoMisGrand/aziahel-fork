Fixed: No way into the Fire School in the Dragonspyre Academy
Forged in Fire sends you to the Fire School to use the forge, but the Academy had no door into it, so nobody could finish the quest. A door now stands by the Fire School's doorway, where you land when you come out of the school. Press X on it to go in; the school's own exit brings you back out beside it. The doorway's dragon flight to Dragon's Roost is unchanged.
Where: Dragonspyre Academy, at the bottom of the Academy by the Fire School.
Test: on Forged in Fire, walk to the Fire School's doorway in the Academy, press X on the door, and use the forge inside.
