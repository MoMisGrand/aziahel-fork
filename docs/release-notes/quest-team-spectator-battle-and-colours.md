New: Quest Team Spectator shows battles and school colours
In a battle, the Quest Team Spectator now shows a spells box for the watched wizard: its hand, pips and health, the card it picks and why, what it threw away, and the enemies and allies. The Quest Team map's border takes the watched wizard's school colour: blue for Freya (Ice), green for Terra (Life), grey for Anubis (Death) and purple for Chaac (Storm). Ultimus's spectator is not changed.
Where: Ahziael, Play, Overlay, Add-ons, Niduus's add-ons, Quest Team Spectator. Click Get the newer one to get version 2 with the spells box.
Test: Watch the Quest Team in a battle. The spells box shows that wizard's hand and casts each round, and Next member switches both the box and the map's border colour.
