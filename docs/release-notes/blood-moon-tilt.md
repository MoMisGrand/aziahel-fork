New: Tilt NPCs and objects from Blood Moon
Blood Moon can now tip a thing forward or back and lean it to one side, not only turn it, and your server shows the tilt in the game straight away. It works for the game's own NPCs and objects and for the ones added in the Customs tab or made in Blood Moon. A thing that was never tilted stays upright as before.
Where: Blood Moon, Rotate (the red and green rings), with Live to server on.
Test: Open the Commons in Blood Moon, turn on Live to server, choose a lamp post, drag the red ring a little and check in the game that the lamp post leans.
